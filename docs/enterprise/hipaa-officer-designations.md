Effective Date: 2026-08-01

## 1. Purpose

This document formally designates the HIPAA Security Officer and HIPAA Privacy
Officer for Pixelated Empathy, as required by the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR
§164.308(a)(2)) and Privacy Rule (45 CFR §164.530(a)).

These designations establish clear accountability for HIPAA compliance and
ensure that individuals with appropriate authority and expertise are responsible
for implementing and maintaining HIPAA safeguards.

---

## 2. HIPAA Security Officer

### 2.1 Designation

**Designated Individual:** Chad (interim) **Title:** HIPAA Security Officer
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01 **Reports To:** Chief Security Officer (CSO) /
Leadership Team

### 2.2 Role Summary

The HIPAA Security Officer is responsible for the development, implementation,
and maintenance of security policies, procedures, and technical safeguards to
protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected
health information (ePHI) created, received, maintained, or transmitted by
Pixelated Empathy.

### 2.3 Responsibilities

**Policy and Procedure Development:**

- Develop and maintain HIPAA Security Rule policies and procedures.
- Ensure policies address all required security standards (§164.308-318).
- Review and update policies annually or when material changes occur.

**Risk Analysis and Management:**

- Conduct and document HIPAA risk analyses (PHI-focused).
- Identify threats and vulnerabilities to ePHI.
- Implement risk mitigation measures.
- Maintain the HIPAA risk register.

**Security Safeguards:**

- Implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for ePHI.
- Ensure encryption standards meet or exceed HIPAA requirements.
- Manage access controls for systems containing ePHI.
- Oversee audit logging and monitoring for ePHI access.

**Workforce Training:**

- Develop and deliver HIPAA security training for all workforce members.
- Ensure training completion is tracked and documented.
- Provide role-specific training for personnel with elevated ePHI access.

**Incident Response:**

- Lead security incident response for ePHI breaches.
- Conduct breach risk assessments (4-factor test per §164.402).
- Coordinate breach notification to HHS and affected individuals.
- Document all incidents and remediation actions.

**Business Associate Management:**

- Identify vendors requiring Business Associate Agreements (BAAs).
- Coordinate BAA execution with legal counsel.
- Monitor vendor compliance with BAA terms.
- Conduct periodic vendor security reviews.

**Audit and Compliance:**

- Prepare for and support HIPAA audits (internal and external).
- Maintain HIPAA compliance documentation.
- Respond to HHS Office for Civil Rights (OCR) inquiries.
- Track and remediate audit findings.

**Continuous Monitoring:**

- Monitor security controls for effectiveness.
- Conduct periodic security assessments.
- Stay current on HIPAA regulatory changes and guidance.
- Report security posture to leadership quarterly.

### 2.4 Authority

The HIPAA Security Officer has authority to:

- Approve or reject vendor BAA requests.
- Require remediation of security vulnerabilities affecting ePHI.
- Suspend access to systems containing ePHI in response to security incidents.
- Engage external security consultants or auditors (with CSO approval).
- Escalate security concerns directly to leadership or legal counsel.

### 2.5 Qualifications

The designated HIPAA Security Officer possesses:

- Knowledge of HIPAA Security Rule requirements.
- Experience with information security frameworks (NIST, ISO 27001, SOC2).
- Understanding of technical safeguards (encryption, access controls, audit
  logging).
- Ability to conduct risk assessments and develop mitigation strategies.
- Strong communication skills for training and incident response.

## 3. HIPAA Privacy Officer

### 3.1 Designation

**Designated Individual:** Chad (interim) **Title:** HIPAA Privacy Officer
**Effective Date:** 2026-08-01 **Reports To:** Chief Security Officer (CSO) /
Leadership Team

### 3.2 Role Summary

The HIPAA Privacy Officer is responsible for ensuring that Pixelated Empathy's
use and disclosure of protected health information (PHI) complies with the HIPAA
Privacy Rule (45 CFR §164.500-534). This includes managing consent, honoring
individual rights, and ensuring minimum necessary standards are followed.

### 3.3 Responsibilities

**Privacy Policy and Procedures:**

- Develop and maintain HIPAA Privacy Rule policies and procedures.
- Ensure policies address use, disclosure, and individual rights.
- Review and update policies annually or when material changes occur.

**Notice of Privacy Practices (NPP):**

- Develop and maintain the Notice of Privacy Practices.
- Ensure NPP is provided to all individuals at first contact.
- Post NPP on Pixelated Empathy website and in physical locations (if
  applicable).
- Update NPP when material changes occur and notify affected individuals.

**Consent and Authorization Management:**

- Establish procedures for obtaining patient consent for treatment, payment, and
  healthcare operations (TPO).
- Manage authorization forms for uses/disclosures requiring individual
  authorization (non-TPO uses).
- Track consent and authorization status for all individuals.
- Ensure consent can be revoked and revocation is honored.

**Individual Rights:**

- Establish procedures for individuals to exercise their rights:
  - Right to access their PHI (§164.524).
  - Right to amend their PHI (§164.526).
  - Right to an accounting of disclosures (§164.528).
  - Right to request restrictions on uses/disclosures (§164.522).
  - Right to confidential communications (§164.522).
  - Right to file complaints (§164.530).
- Respond to individual rights requests within required timelines (30 days, with
  one 30-day extension if needed).
- Document all rights requests and responses.

**Minimum Necessary Standard:**

- Implement minimum necessary policies for uses and disclosures of PHI.
- Ensure workforce members access only the PHI needed for their job functions.
- Review access patterns to identify potential over-access.
- Update role-based access controls to enforce minimum necessary.

**Privacy Incident Response:**

- Lead privacy incident response for unauthorized uses/disclosures of PHI.
- Conduct breach risk assessments (4-factor test per §164.402).
- Coordinate breach notification to HHS and affected individuals.
- Document all privacy incidents and remediation actions.

**Workforce Training:**

- Develop and deliver HIPAA privacy training for all workforce members.
- Ensure training covers use/disclosure rules, individual rights, and minimum
  necessary.
- Track training completion and provide refresher training as needed.

**Complaint Management:**

- Establish procedures for receiving and documenting privacy complaints.
- Investigate all complaints (from individuals or workforce members).
- Document investigation results and remediation actions.
- Prohibit retaliation against complainants.

**Audit and Compliance:**

- Prepare for and support HIPAA privacy audits (internal and external).
- Maintain privacy compliance documentation.
- Respond to HHS OCR inquiries related to privacy.
- Track and remediate privacy audit findings.

**Continuous Monitoring:**

- Monitor compliance with privacy policies and procedures.
- Conduct periodic privacy assessments.
- Stay current on HIPAA regulatory changes and guidance.
- Report privacy posture to leadership quarterly.

### 3.4 Authority

The HIPAA Privacy Officer has authority to:

- Approve or reject requests for PHI use/disclosure outside TPO.
- Require remediation of privacy policy violations.
- Suspend access to PHI in response to privacy incidents or over-access.
- Engage external privacy consultants or auditors (with CSO approval).
- Escalate privacy concerns directly to leadership or legal counsel.

### 3.5 Qualifications

The designated HIPAA Privacy Officer possesses:

- Knowledge of HIPAA Privacy Rule requirements.
- Understanding of healthcare operations and TPO uses.
- Experience with consent management and individual rights procedures.
- Ability to interpret privacy regulations and develop compliant procedures.
- Strong communication skills for training and complaint investigation.

## 4. Coordination Between Officers

The HIPAA Security Officer and Privacy Officer coordinate on:

**Incident Response:**

- Security Officer leads technical containment and forensic investigation.
- Privacy Officer leads breach determination and individual notification.
- Both officers participate in post-incident review.

**Risk Analysis:**

- Security Officer focuses on technical threats and vulnerabilities.
- Privacy Officer focuses on use/disclosure risks and individual harm.
- Both officers contribute to the comprehensive HIPAA risk analysis.

**Training:**

- Security Officer delivers technical safeguard training.
- Privacy Officer delivers use/disclosure and individual rights training.
- Both officers ensure all workforce members complete both training modules.

**Vendor Management:**

- Security Officer reviews vendor security controls.
- Privacy Officer reviews vendor data handling and BAA terms.
- Both officers approve vendor onboarding for vendors handling PHI.

**Audit Preparation:**

- Security Officer prepares security safeguards documentation.
- Privacy Officer prepares use/disclosure and individual rights documentation.
- Both officers coordinate audit response and remediation.

## 5. Delegation and Succession

### 5.1 Delegation

The HIPAA Security Officer and Privacy Officer may delegate specific tasks to
qualified workforce members, but retain ultimate responsibility for compliance.

Delegated tasks must be documented, and delegates must receive appropriate
training and authority to perform their duties.

### 5.2 Succession Planning

If the designated officer is unavailable (vacation, illness, departure):

**Interim Designation:**

- The CSO designates an interim officer from qualified workforce members.
- Interim designation is documented and communicated to leadership.
- Interim officer has full authority and responsibility during the absence.

**Permanent Replacement:**

- If an officer departs, the CSO designates a permanent replacement within 30
  days.
- Replacement must meet qualification requirements.
- Replacement receives HIPAA officer training within 14 days.
- This document is updated to reflect the new designation.

- Breach notifications and investigation reports.
- Individual rights requests and responses.
- Complaints and investigation results.
- Business Associate Agreements.
- Audit reports and remediation plans.
- This designation document (and all updates).

**Storage:**

- Documentation stored in `docs/compliance/hipaa/` (version-controlled).
- Sensitive documents (breach reports, individual requests) stored in
  access-controlled location with audit logging.
- Backup copies maintained per disaster recovery plan.
