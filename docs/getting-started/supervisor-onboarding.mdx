## What You'll Learn

In this tutorial, you'll register your organisation on Pixelated Empathy,
configure your first training programme, invite trainees, and monitor their
progress. By the end, you'll have a live training cohort with trainees enrolled.

**Time to complete:** approximately 15 minutes, plus time for trainee
registration.

---

## Step 1: Register Your Organisation

Contact
[enterprise@pixelatedempathy.com](mailto:enterprise@pixelatedempathy.com) or
visit the [Enterprise Portal](https://pixelatedempathy.com/enterprise).

You'll need:

- Organisation name and type
- Estimated trainee count
- Compliance requirements (HIPAA, FERPA, IRB)
- SSO/identity provider details (if applicable)

After registration, you'll receive admin credentials and access to the
**Supervisor Dashboard**.

---

## Step 2: Configure Your Training Programme

From the Supervisor Dashboard, set up your training programme:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Create trainee cohorts" icon="users">
    Organise by class year, specialisation, or rotation. Each cohort gets:
    - Required scenario sequence
    - Competency benchmarks
    - Assessment frequency
    - Difficulty levels

    **Path:** Dashboard → Cohorts → Create New Cohort

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Assign training pathways" icon="route">
    **Built-in programmes:**
    - *Basic Therapeutic Skills* — 5 progressive sessions for beginners
    - *Crisis Intervention Specialist* — 8 complex scenarios for experienced clinicians
    - *Custom Pathway* — Define your own sequence

    **Path:** Dashboard → Training Programmes → Assign to Cohort

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Set competency thresholds" icon="chart-bar">
    Minimum levels across 8 core skills:

    | Skill                     | Description                | Levels |
    | ------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------ |
    | Rapport Building          | Connection & alliance      | 1-5    |
    | Active Listening          | Understanding & reflection | 1-5    |
    | Empathy Demonstration     | Emotional attunement       | 1-5    |
    | Crisis Management         | Safety assessment          | 1-5    |
    | Resistance Handling       | Therapeutic resistance     | 1-5    |
    | Boundary Setting          | Professional limits        | 1-5    |
    | Therapeutic Confrontation | Appropriate challenging    | 1-5    |
    | Cultural Competence       | Diverse backgrounds        | 1-5    |

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Notice that competency thresholds cascade — a trainee who falls below the
threshold in any skill will be flagged for additional training in that area,
even if their other skills are strong.

---

## Step 3: Invite Trainees

1. Navigate to Dashboard → Cohorts → [Cohort Name] → Members
2. Click **Invite Members**
3. Enter emails or upload a CSV
4. Select role and training pathway
5. Send invitations

Trainees auto-enrol upon registration and can begin sessions immediately.

---

## Step 4: Monitor Trainee Progress

Once trainees start sessions, you'll have real-time visibility:

- **Live Observation** — Watch sessions with competency overlay
- **Intervention Alerts** — Notifications for skill deficits
- **Progress Reports** — Analytics, patterns, breakthroughs
- **Development Plans** — Personalised growth recommendations

<Info>
  All data is encrypted and access-controlled. See [Compliance &
  Security](/reference/compliance/security) for details.
</Info>

Try observing a live session from your dashboard. You'll see the competency
scores update in real time as the trainee responds to the AI client.
