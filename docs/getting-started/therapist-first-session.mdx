## What You'll Learn

In this tutorial, you'll create a Pixelated Empathy account, set up your
clinical profile, run your first AI training session, and read your feedback
report. By the end, you'll have completed one full training cycle in The Empathy
Gym.

**Time to complete:** approximately 5 minutes.

<Note>
  If your organisation has already set up Pixelated Empathy, ask your admin for
  an **invitation link** — it auto-connects you to your institution's training
  programme.
</Note>

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Navigate to [pixelatedempathy.com](https://pixelatedempathy.com) and click **Get
Started**.

You'll need:

- Email or institutional SSO
- Professional role (therapist, counsellor, psychologist, student)
- Years of clinical experience
- Areas of specialisation (optional)

Fill in your details and verify your email. You'll be taken to the dashboard.

---

## Step 2: Complete Your Clinical Profile

Calibrate training scenarios to your experience level:

1. **Select training focus** — Crisis intervention, trauma-informed care,
   motivational interviewing, couples therapy, substance abuse
2. **Set difficulty** — Beginner (guided) through Master (complex, multi-issue)
3. **Choose feedback mode** — Real-time, post-session, or both

<Info>
  You can update your profile anytime via **Settings → Clinical Profile**. The
  platform adapts as you grow.
</Info>

Notice that the difficulty setting affects how the AI client responds — Beginner
scenarios provide more guidance and fewer simultaneous challenges, while Master
scenarios present complex, multi-issue situations that test your ability to
prioritise.

---

## Step 3: Launch Your First Session

Click **Enter The Empathy Gym** from your dashboard.

1. **Pick a scenario** — Anxiety, depression, trauma, crisis, personality
   disorders
2. **Review the client profile** — Emotional baseline, personality traits,
   triggers, goals
3. **Start the conversation** — Text or voice. The AI client adapts dynamically.

<Warning>
  Crisis scenarios include sensitive content with built-in safety protocols. You
  can skip or adjust content preferences before starting.
</Warning>

Try responding to the client's opening statement. Don't worry about being
perfect — the platform is designed to help you learn through practice. Notice
how the AI client responds differently based on what you say and how you say it.

---

## Step 4: Review Your Feedback

After the session, you'll receive a comprehensive post-session report:

| Metric                   | Description                          |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| **Empathy Score**        | Emotional attunement (0.0–1.0)       |
| **Technique Analysis**   | CBT, DBT, MI, psychodynamic usage    |
| **Bias Check**           | Demographic bias pattern detection   |
| **Crisis Response**      | Crisis signal identification speed   |
| **Therapeutic Alliance** | Rapport strength (task, bond, goals) |
| **Skill Progression**    | Historical performance comparison    |

Click any metric to see specific examples from the conversation and
evidence-based improvement suggestions.

<Tip>
  Your first session won't be perfect — that's the point. Review the feedback,
  note one area to improve, and try another session. The Empathy Gym is about
  repetition and gradual improvement.
</Tip>
