## The Science Behind Pixelated Empathy

Pixelated Empathy is built on established psychological science and modern AI.
The concepts documented here explain how the platform models emotions, evaluates
therapeutic communication, detects bias, and monitors for crisis signals.

Understanding these concepts helps you interpret feedback reports, make sense of
scores, and understand the design decisions behind the platform.

<Note>
  Every framework described here is grounded in peer-reviewed research. Where
  we've adapted or extended a framework for our platform, we note it explicitly.
</Note>

## How It All Fits Together

These concepts do not operate in isolation. During every training session in The
Empathy Gym, they work together:

1. The AI client's **Big Five personality** drives their behavioural patterns
   and emotional responses
2. **Plutchik's Wheel** classifies the emotional states that emerge during the
   conversation
3. **Emotion scoring** quantifies intensity, valence, and arousal in real time
4. The **EARS framework** evaluates the therapeutic quality of your responses
5. **Therapeutic alliance** tracks the evolving strength of the relationship
6. **Conversational dynamics** measure the flow, reciprocity, and rhythm of the
   dialogue
7. **Crisis detection** monitors for safety-critical signals throughout
8. **Bias detection** ensures equitable care across all demographics

Together, these systems provide a comprehensive, multi-dimensional view of
therapeutic competence — turning every practice session into a rich learning
experience.
