## What Is the AI Engine?

The **AI Engine** is the cognitive core of Pixelated Empathy. It is a Python-based environment for model research, training, and inference — handling emotion detection, therapeutic response generation, AI client simulation, response evaluation, and real-time crisis monitoring.

The engine is maintained as a submodule of the main Pixelated repository and runs as a separate service during development and production.

<Info>
  **Repository:** [github.com/daggerstuff/ai](https://github.com/daggerstuff/ai) (submodule of [daggerstuff/pixelated](https://github.com/daggerstuff/pixelated))
</Info>

## What It Does

### Emotion Detection

The engine classifies emotional states in real time using a multi-label approach based on **Plutchik's Wheel of Emotions**. Each detected emotion is characterised by intensity (`0.0`–`1.0`), valence (`-1.0`–`1.0`), and arousal (`0.0`–`1.0`). See [Emotion Model](/concepts/emotion-model) for the scoring system.

### Therapeutic Response Generation

The engine generates responses that demonstrate the **EARS framework** — Empathise, Acknowledge, Reflect, Support — and scores the therapist's responses for therapeutic quality. See [EARS Framework](/concepts/ears-framework).

### AI Client Simulation

The engine simulates clients with **Big Five (OCEAN)** personality profiles that drive consistent behavioural patterns, emotional responses, resistance patterns, and trust progression. See [Personality Model](/concepts/personality-model).

### Crisis Monitoring

Real-time monitoring across five crisis signal categories (self-harm, violence, despair, substance abuse, medical) with five-level risk classification and severity modifiers. See [Crisis Signals](/concepts/crisis-signals).

### Bias Detection

The engine monitors for 40+ bias categories during sessions, computing demographic parity, equalised odds, and treatment equality as fairness metrics. See [Bias Types](/concepts/bias-types).

## Training Pipeline

The engine includes infrastructure for model training:

- **SFT (Supervised Fine-Tuning)** — Training on therapeutic conversation datasets
- **DPO (Direct Preference Optimisation)** — Aligning model responses to clinical preferences
- **GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimisation)** — Group-based reinforcement learning
- **Safety checkers** — Multilingual and clinical safety validators
- **Reward scoring** — Scoring therapeutic quality of generated responses
- **Coverage gates** — 95% safety-critical coverage, 40% pilot coverage thresholds

<Warning>
  Model training runs CPU-only PyTorch in CI. Production inference may use GPU acceleration.
</Warning>
